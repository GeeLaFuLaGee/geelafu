GEE LA FU

Drawing is where it started and where it always returns. No sketches, no preparation — the hand moves and the image appears. What you see here is intuitive line work built over decades, influenced by street art, doodle culture and a stubborn refusal to stay amateur. Gee La Fu is the artistic alter ego of a Belgian designer who draws the way others think.
